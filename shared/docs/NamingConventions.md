- Constants: `Roles.js`, `Districts.js`
- Enums: `CasePriority.js`, `UserStatus.js`
- DTOs: `LoginRequest.js`, `PredictionResponse.js`
- Interfaces: `IRepository.js`, `IFIRService.js`
- Helpers: `DateHelper.js`, `GeoHelper.js`
- Mappers: `FIRMapper.js`, `PredictionMapper.js`
- Errors: `AppError.js`, `ValidationError.js`
